How it pays out

The terms, kept simple.

No points, no tiers, no waiting on a payout. Just account credit, applied for you.

One reward per referral

Each person you refer earns you one credit when they first subscribe. Refer ten people, stack ten rewards.

Applied as account credit

Credit is netted off your own Warmbly invoices automatically. It is not a cash payout, and there is nothing to claim.

30-day clawback

If a referred subscription is refunded or cancelled inside 30 days, the matching credit is reversed. Genuine signups keep theirs.

Fair use

Self-referrals, duplicate accounts, and other abuse do not qualify. We review rewards before they apply.
